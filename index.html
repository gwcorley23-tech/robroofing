Insurance Claims, Done Honestly

We never waive your deductible — because the law is clear.

In Texas, it's against the law for contractors to waive, absorb, or credit your insurance deductible. We protect your claim, your coverage, and your future by doing things the right way.

It's the law in Texas.

Texas Insurance Code §542.052 prohibits contractors from waiving or rebating any portion of an insurance deductible. Doing so is considered insurance fraud — and it can put you, your contractor, and your coverage at serious risk. We'll never ask you to.

✓ The Right Way

  • You pay your deductible — protecting your claim and your home.
  • Your coverage stays valid and your insurer stays on your side.
  • We walk you through the entire claim, start to finish.
  • Everything is documented with honest, photo-backed assessments.

✗ The Wrong Way

  • "Waiving" your deductible may seem helpful now…
  • …but it can cost you everything later.
  • Denied claims, liability for the full cost, lost insurability.
  • If it sounds too good to be true, it probably is — and probably illegal.
